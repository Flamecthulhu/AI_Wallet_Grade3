\maketitle
    $$Z = \sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_{i} \cdot w_{i})+b_{i}$$
    ${Z}$ is the linear combination and is not activated.
    $x_{i}$ is your input (either a vector or an array).\\
    $w_{i}$ is the weight for each input.
    $b_{i}$ is the bias for each neuron node.
    $$A=f(Z)$$
    $A$ is activated.
    $f(\cdot)$ is activation function.\\
    Activation functions:\\[10pt]
    Tanh:$$tanh(z)=\frac{e^z-e^{-z}}{e^z+e^{-z}}$$
    ReLU:$$f(z)=max(0,z)$$
    Sigmoid:$$\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}$$
    Softmax:$$Softmax(z_i)=\frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}e^{z_j}}$$
    Forward propagation\\
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Calculating $Z$ (linear combination of inputs)
        \item Applying activation function $A = f(Z)$
        \item Repeating layer-by-layer until final prediction $\hat{y}$
    \end{itemize}
    In layer L:\\ 
    Weight: $W^{(L)}$, Biases: $b^{(L)}$, Non-activated combination: $Z^{(L)}$,\\
    Activated output: $A^{(L)}$, Activation function: $f^{(L)}(\cdot)$.\\
    Input: $$x^{(L_{in})}$$
    Output:$$Z^{(L_{out})}=A^{(L_{hidden})}\times w^{(L_{out})}+b^{(L_{out})}$$
    $$\hat{y}=f^{(L_{out})}(Z^{(L_{out})})$$
    $\hat{y}$ is model predict output.\\
    Loss function:\\[10pt]
    Mean Squared Error, MSE
    $$L(y,\hat{y})=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2$$
    Binary Cross-Entropy (for sigmoid output): \\
    $$L(y,\hat{y})=-\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}[y_i\ln(\hat{y}_i)+(1-y_i)\ln(1-\hat{y}_i)]$$
    Categorical Cross-Entropy (for softmax output): \\
    $$L(y,\hat{y})=-\sum_{i=1}^{m}(y_i\times\ln(\hat{y}_i))$$\\
    Backward propagation\\
    Output Gradient
    $$\delta^{(L_{out})}=\frac{\partial L}{\partial A^{(L_{out})}}\times f^{(L_{out})}(Z^{(L_{out})})$$
    $$\frac{\partial L}{\partial W^{(L_{out})}}=A^{(L_{hidden})T}\times\delta^{(L_{out})}$$
    $$\frac{\partial L}{\partial b^{(L_{out})}}=sum(\delta^{(L_{out})},axis=0)$$\\
    Hidden Gradient
    $$\delta^{(L-1)}=\delta^{(L)}\times (W^{(L)})^T\times f^{(L-1)}(Z^{(L-1)})$$
    $$\frac{\partial L}{\partial W^{(L-1)}}=A^{(L-1)T}\times\delta^{(L-1)}$$
    $$\frac{\partial L}{\partial b^{(L-1)}}=sum(\delta^{(L-1)},axis=0)$$\\
    Update weights and biases(Gradient Descent)
    $$W^{(L)}_{new}=W^{(L)}_{old}-learning\_rate\times\frac{\partial L}{\partial W^{(L)}}$$
    $$b^{(L)}_{new}=b^{(L)}_{old}-learning\_rate\times\frac{\partial L}{\partial b^{(L)}}$$
